Getting started

The basics, if you're just getting set up.
How do I post?

Tap the + button on the Home tab and take a photo of your pet — daily posts are camera-only, to keep things real rather than a curated photo dump.

What's a Baowstreak?

Your daily posting streak. Post once a day to keep it going — the number and ring on your Profile track how many days in a row you've kept it up, and you'll unlock new titles as it grows.

Why does my feed only show a few pets?

Pawrsure is built around your actual neighborhood, not a global feed — you'll see posts from people genuinely nearby. If your area doesn't have much activity yet, the feed automatically searches a bit further out to find enough to show you.

Account

Managing your account and data.
How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and everything tied to it — it can't be undone, so make sure before confirming.

I signed up with email — where's my confirmation link?

Check your spam or promotions folder if it hasn't arrived after a few minutes. If it's genuinely missing, email us and we'll help sort it out.

Can I change my username?

Not yet from within the app — email us and we can help directly. Note that short usernames (5 characters or fewer) go through a quick manual review before they're active, to keep them from being squatted on.

Shelters & Organizers

For rescue accounts and event hosts.
I registered my shelter — why can't I post a listing yet?

Shelter accounts are reviewed by hand before they're approved, usually quickly. You'll be able to post adoptable listings as soon as that's done.

Why did my first event get blocked?

The first event from any new organizer account goes through a quick manual approval, same as shelters — after that, you can create events freely up to your plan's monthly limit.

Report a problem

Found something that isn't working, or content that shouldn't be there?

Use the report option directly on a post or comment for anything that violates our guidelines, or email us for anything else — bugs, account issues, or general feedback. We read every message.
