Privacy Policy

Last updated August 10, 2026

Pawrsure is built around a simple idea: your neighborhood, not the whole world. That same thinking shapes how we handle your data — we collect what's needed to connect you with real pets and people nearby, and we've deliberately limited what gets shown or shared.

What we collect

  • Account info — your email address (or your Apple/Google account identifier if you sign in that way), a username, and a display name.
  • Pet info — your pet's name, species, breed, and photos you choose to upload.
  • Location — your device's location is used to find pets, posts, and events near you. See "How we handle location" below for the important part.
  • Posts and activity — photos, captions, comments, likes, and your daily posting streak.
  • Subscription status — if you subscribe to an organizer tier, Apple's payment system handles the transaction; we only see whether you're subscribed, never your payment details.
  • Notification preferences — if you enable reminders, your device receives them locally; we don't track whether you've opened them.

How we handle location

This is the part we care about most. Your precise location is used on our servers to figure out what's nearby — but it is never shown precisely to anyone, including other users. Distances appear only as broad bands ("Under 1km away," "3–5km away," and so on), specifically so no one can use a post's distance and timestamp to work out exactly where you or your pet actually are.

Why this matters Precise pet locations can be misused to target valuable animals. Pawrsure is built to make that meaningfully harder by design, not just by policy.

Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers to run Pawrsure, and we don't sell your data to anyone:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and photo storage.
  • Resend — sends account-related emails, like confirming your address.
  • Apple and Google — if you choose to sign in with either, they handle that authentication directly.
  • Apple's StoreKit — processes any subscription payments; we never see your card details.

Your account, your data

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account. This is a real, complete deletion — it removes your account and everything tied to it, not just a deactivation.

Children's privacy

Pawrsure isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.
